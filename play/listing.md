## Full description

CrewTally helps crew supervisors keep track of who worked, what they earned, and what they
have been paid. It is built for the field: everything runs on your phone, offline, with no
account to create and no internet connection required.

Keep a simple record of your work:

- Companies you work for and the projects under each one.
- Your crew (clerks), with an optional phone number for each.
- Daily attendance, one tap per person, present or absent.
- Extra pay lines for a day, such as lunch, transport, or a bonus, and deductions when you
  need them.
- Payments you make to each person, with the outstanding balance always calculated for you.

Made to be fast and clear on a busy site:

- The daily attendance loop takes seconds: open the day, mark everyone present, then tap the
  few exceptions.
- Balances are worked out automatically from days worked, rates, extras, and payments. You
  never add them up by hand.
- Each project keeps its own currency as a label, so amounts always show with the right
  symbol.
- Large text and large buttons, built to stay readable and easy to tap.

Your data stays yours:

- CrewTally is offline only. Your companies, crew, attendance, and payments never leave your
  device.
- No account, no sign in, no advertising, no tracking.
- You can export a full backup to a single file whenever you want and share it wherever you
  choose. CrewTally never uploads it on its own.
- Searching your phone contacts to fill in a name and number is optional. If you allow it,
  contact details are read only at that moment to show suggestions, and nothing from your
  contacts is stored or sent.

CrewTally does one job and does it plainly: attendance and pay tracking for crew supervisors,
without the overhead of accounts or the cloud.
