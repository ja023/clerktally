## CrewTally Privacy Policy

CrewTally is an offline app for tracking crew attendance and pay. This policy explains, in
plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

### Data storage

CrewTally is an offline app. Everything you enter, including companies, clerks, projects,
attendance, and payments, stays on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

CrewTally does not collect or transmit any data. It has no account system, no advertising, and
no analytics. The app does not have permission to access the internet.

### Contacts

When you search your contacts to fill in a clerk or company's name and phone number, CrewTally
reads that contact information only at that moment, only to show matching suggestions. Nothing
from your contacts is stored, sent, or used for any other purpose.

Using contact search is optional. If you do not grant contacts permission, the name and phone
fields still work as normal text fields.

### Backups

You can export a backup of your data at any time from More, then Backup, and share that file
however you choose. CrewTally never uploads it anywhere on its own. Once you share a backup
file, how it is stored and who can access it is controlled by the app or service you shared it
with, not by CrewTally.

### Children

CrewTally is a tool for work record keeping and is not directed at children.
